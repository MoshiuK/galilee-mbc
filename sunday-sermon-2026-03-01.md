## Title: "Faith When Life Gets Hard"

## Scripture Texts
- **Primary:** James 1:2–4 (KJV)
- **Supporting:** Psalm 34:17–19, Mark 4:35–41, Romans 8:28

---

## Opening Prayer Theme
Lord, strengthen our faith. Help us trust You even when we can't trace You.

---

## Introduction (3–5 min)
Life doesn't pause because we believe. Bills still come. Sickness still knocks. Relationships still break. But faith isn't the absence of trouble — it's the presence of God IN the trouble.

**Illustration:** A tree doesn't grow strong roots in calm weather. It's the wind that forces the roots deeper.

---

## Point 1: Trials Are Tests, Not Punishments (James 1:2–4)
> "Count it all joy when ye fall into divers temptations; knowing this, that the trying of your faith worketh patience."

- God isn't punishing you — He's positioning you.
- The trial has a purpose: patience, maturity, completeness.
- Joy doesn't mean happiness about the pain. Joy means confidence in the outcome.

**Application:** Stop asking "Why me?" and start asking "What are You building in me?"

---

## Point 2: God Is Close to the Broken (Psalm 34:17–19)
> "The LORD is nigh unto them that are of a broken heart; and saveth such as be of a contrite spirit."

- When you feel furthest from God, He's actually closest.
- Many afflictions — but delivered out of them ALL.
- God doesn't always remove the storm, but He always shows up in it.

**Illustration:** A parent doesn't leave a sick child alone. They sit closer. God does the same.

---

## Point 3: Jesus Is in Your Boat (Mark 4:35–41)
> "And he arose, and rebuked the wind, and said unto the sea, Peace, be still."

- The disciples panicked. Jesus was asleep. Not because He didn't care — because He wasn't worried.
- The same Jesus who calmed the sea is in YOUR situation right now.
- His question to them: "Why are ye so fearful? How is it that ye have no faith?"

**Application:** Faith isn't screaming louder in the storm. It's remembering who's in the boat with you.

---

## Point 4: It's Working for Your Good (Romans 8:28)
> "And we know that all things work together for good to them that love God..."

- Not some things. ALL things.
- Even the things that hurt. Even the things that don't make sense yet.
- God is a master builder. He uses every piece — even the broken ones.

---

## Altar Call / Invitation
- If you're going through something hard right now — come to this altar.
- If your faith is shaking — bring it to God. He won't reject honest struggle.
- If you need to rededicate your life — today is the day.
